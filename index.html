ABOUT HEARTBRUSH

Connection is part of care.

Through volunteering in hospital settings, I have seen the immense pressure carried by healthcare teams and the loneliness that patients and families can feel during difficult moments. Wellbeing has many parts, and while medicine treats the body, the spirit and emotions also require support.

That is why I founded HeartBrush. We bring art experiences to bedside tables, breakrooms, and care spaces, turning sterile moments into opportunities for calm, creativity, and connection. Whether someone is a patient, loved one, or provider, my hope is that HeartBrush offers a small moment of peace when it is needed most.

— Lipi Kothavade, Founder

THE HEARTBRUSH TEAM

People behind

Our team is a community of students who are dedicated to making breakrooms and bedsides places full of color and creativity.

FOUNDER

Lipi Kothavade

A sophomore at Woodbridge Academy, an allied health school, Lipi is an artist who aspires to be a medical professional.

More than a year of volunteering in hospitals showed her how much a small creative moment can mean in a clinical space — and HeartBrush grew from there.

WHAT WE DO

Small moments.
Meaningful impact.

Our organization seeks to bring people joy amidst their struggles, through small actions, wherever we can.

✦

Patient art sessions

We plan to offer patients the opportunity to create art right from their bedside tables. They don’t have to be big or beautiful, but they provide a calm in the storm, and are a reminder of their resilience and hope.

Learn more →
☀

Care team wellness

Healthcare providers are the people working overtime behind the scenes, and it often takes a toll on them. These art sessions offer them a chance to step back from the high stress environment they are surrounded by.

Learn more →
♥

Family involvement

While family members and loved ones may not be the ones in the bed, oftentimes they’re also going through their own challenges and struggles. They also deserve a chance to relax and take a break, as part of our community.
