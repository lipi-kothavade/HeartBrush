ABOUT HEARTBRUSH

Connection is part of care.

HeartBrush was imagined as a softer corner of the hospital: a place where a blank page, a few colors, and a caring guide can help someone feel more like themselves.

We partner with hospitals to make creative wellness accessible for everyone who needs a moment to reset.
