HEARTBRUSH PROGRAM

Family involvement.

A calm, creative pause for the loved ones supporting patients.

What

We believe families and loved ones also deserve a chance to destress, and we hope to provide them with that opportunity.

Why

While they may not be the ones in the hospital bed, they are often also struggling. Many relatives experience conditions or at least symptoms of disorders such as Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder, Anxiety, and Depression. When family members are in the hospital, they may experience spikes in cortisol, the body’s stress hormone, levels. Emotions can range from fear, to anger, to hopelessness. While the concern for a loved one will always exist, we want to create opportunities for family members to relax as well.

How

We plan to provide simple art kits for waiting rooms, stocked with activities that can be done while visiting. These can be done while spending time with the patient, or while sitting in the waiting room.
